SUBJECT : Monthly Report - -Jordan Bureau- -December 1985 


I . OPERATIONS 

A . Monitorial /Editorial 

1. Jordanian-Syrian Relations continued their turn for the 
better as King Husayn paid his first visit to Damascus since *1979 
on December 30-31. Although no joint communique was issued on 
Husayn’ s talks with Syrian President Al-Asad, Jordanian Foreign 
Minister Al-Masri was quoted afterward as saying the visit was 
"highly successful." A Jordanian "source" cited by Radio Monte 
Carlo said the two sides avoid a communique so that they would 
not have to mention contentious issues such as the Iraq- Iran war 
and relations with the PLO. PLO leader 'Arafat, meanwhile, spent 
much of the month "ill" in Tunis. 

2. On the 29th the bureau processed the 51-minute text of 
the tripartite agreement on Lebanon signed in the Syrian capital 
the previous day. In the agreement, broadcast by Damascus radio, 
the Lebanese Forces, the Amal movement and the Progressive Social- 
ist Party supported an end to sectarianism, a new constitution 
and government and a final conclusion to the Lebanese civil war. 
The document also allows the stationing of Syrian military units 
at agreed positions in Lebanon and stresses the need for Lebanese- 
Syrian "security integration."
