MEMORANDUM 

TO: Executive Directors 

FROM: O - Idar Rimestad 

SUBJECT: National Interdepartmental Seminar 

The continuing attention of the Executive Directors is 
called to the strict mandate to send all officers 
"assigned to key positions in the underdeveloped world 
or in headquarters positions related thereto" to the 
National Interdepartmental Seminar (FSI Course A-700) . 
Attendance of these designated officers at the Course 
is compulsory unless specific permission is obtained 
from this office. This requirement includes newly 
appointed Ambassadors. 

There has been a recent slippage in observing this 
requirement. I trust it will be strictly adhered to 
in the future. 


cc: The Director General
