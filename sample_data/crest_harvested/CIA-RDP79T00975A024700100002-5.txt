FOR THE RECORD* 


* The Germanies ; The West German constitutional 

court on 18 June turned down the Bavarian state gov- 
ernment's second request in three weeks to block 
ratification of the inter-German basic relations 
treaty. The way is now clear for Bonn and Pankow 
to exchange instruments of ratification tomorrow, 
bringing the treaty into force on 21 June.
