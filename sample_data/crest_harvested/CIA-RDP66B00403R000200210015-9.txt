JOURNAL 


OFFICE OF LEGISLATIVE COUNSEL 


Tuesday - II February 1964 


1. (Unclassified - JSW) Talked with Russell Blandford, House 
Armed Services Committee staff, suggesting that he bring to Chairman 
Vinson's attention the Stewart ALsop article on CIA in the 15 February 
issue of the SATURDAY EVENING POST. Blandford appreciated the 
call and said he would be glad to do so. 

2. (Unclassified - JSW) Talked with William Darden, Senate 
Armed Services Committee staff, suggesting that he bring to Senator 
Russell's attention the Stewart ALsop article on CIA in the 15 February 
issue of the SATURDAY EVENING POST. Darden stated he would be 
glad to do so upon Russell's return to Washington on Monday of next 
week. 


3. (Unclassified - JSW) Talked with Jim Gehrig in the office 
of Senator Stuart Symington, bringing to his attention the SATURDAY 
EVENING POST article by Stewart Alsop. Gehrig appreciated the call 
stating he would bring it to the attention of Senator Symington for 
consideration of introduction into the CONGRESSIONAL RECORD in 
connection with Symington's speech.
