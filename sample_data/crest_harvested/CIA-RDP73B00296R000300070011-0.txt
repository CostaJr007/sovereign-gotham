On July 9 Senator Ribicoff, Chairman of the Subcommittee 
on Executive Reorganization, and Senator Muskie, Chairman 
of the Subcommittee on Intergovernmental Relations, (both 
Subcommittees of the Senate Committee on Government 
Operations) wrote the Secretary requesting that he, along 
with Defense Secretary Laird, CIA Director Helms, and 
Attorney General Mitchell, appear before the two Sub- 
committees . in executive session at the earliest possible 
date to discuss the alleged participation of officials in 
the governments of allied nations in Southeast Asia in the 
heroin traffic infesting American troops in that area* 

On August 11 I telephoned Tyrone Brown, Staff Director 
of the Muskie Subcommittee, to request that he convey to 
the Senators assurances that the Department was carefully 
considering the request contained in the letter of July 9, 
and that the delay in replying was due to the need to 
coordinate with the many Government agencies involved in 
this matter. 

Mr. Brown expressed his appreciation for the call, especially 
because he had drafted the July 9 letter. I went on, to say 
that we were anxious to speak to the Senators at the first
