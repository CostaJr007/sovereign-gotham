25 January 1963 


MEMORANDUM TO ALL SUPERVISORS 


I am very pleased to pass on to you a commendation from the 
President of the United States for the work of the intelligence 
services during the recent Cuban crisis. Mr. McCone's letter to 
me at the time of the presentation on 23 January is also attached. 

I would like each of you to read these two documents to your next 
staff meeting. I have also asked that they be distributed to our 
station and base chiefs, both overseas and domestic. 

Please note that the classification is US OFFICIAL USE ONLY, 
and enjoin your personnel against public discussion. 

We want each and every individual in the organisation to know 
that our work during the Cuban crisis brought approbation from the 
highest level. We hope that this will be an inspiration to one and 
all for even greater efforts in the future. 


Marshall S. Carter 
Lieutenant General, USA 
Deputy Director
