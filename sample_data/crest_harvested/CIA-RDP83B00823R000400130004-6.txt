11 September 1969 

MEMORANDUM FOR: Chief, Executive and Planning Division 

SUBJECT : Significant Accomplishments - FY 1969 


The following information is submitted as the consolidated 
significant accomplishments of the Security Research Staff during 
the fiscal year 1969: 

1. During the reporting period the Security Research Staff 
continued to serve as the counterintelligence arm of the Office of 
Security in developing and implementing defensive programs to 
protect the integrity of the Agency, its operations, its installations 
and its employees by assault by hostile intelligence services and 
elements. 

u/ 2. During the fiscal year 1969 this Staff opened 2,427 
security counterintelligence cases and closed 2, 827. In addition, 
1,127 special investigations (an intensive category of counterintel- 
ligence research) were processed. In fulfilling its security- 
counterintelligence function, this Staff reviewed 17, 082 security 
files, 3*106 intelligence reports and 3,043 communications from 
outside the Agency for counterintelligence research data. As an 
extension of the research activity, this Staff conducted 347 inter- 
views and debriefings. In support of a continuing counterintelligence 
training requirement, 27 lectures on such varied topics as Soviet 
intelligence apparatus. Communism and Subversion in the United 
States, the Internal Threat, Hostile Entrapment Procedures, etc., 
were presented. Counterintelligence liaison and counterintelligence 
source contacts were 1,481 and 697 respectively. Special counter- 
intelligence source checks totalled 3,747 and the Staff responded 
to 1,204 outside inquiries from both Agency components and other 
government agencies. Review and action were accomplished on 
10. 077 news med ia items. A special counterintelligence review of 
[reports was also accomplished during this period. 
Continuing dissemination of the Staff's research product resulted 
in the preparation of 3, 705 memoranda.
