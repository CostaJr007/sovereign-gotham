Possible Fields of Immediate Usefulness 
To assist in one or more of the following activities; 

1. Improvement in the value of Intelligence studies now being made by 
Intelligence Reserve Units, and by contract at universities and elsewhere. 

This improvement to be accomplished by: 

a. Clear definition of the purpose and scope of the work in each case. 

b. Conferences with those doing the work. 

c. Close supervision of progress. 

d. A continuing review of the requirements in changing situations. 

e. Obtaining reports in helpful form in time to be of service. 

f. Discontinuance of unpromising projects. 

g. Inauguration of new projects under promising circumstances. 

2. Procurement of desirable personnel especially on the higher levels. 

3. .Supervision of Schools and Training now being conducted by or for CIA.
