MEMORANDUM FOR: Deputy Director for Administration 


FROM: 

Deputy Chief, DC I History Staff 

SUBJECT: Histories on Laos and Southeast Asia 

for use by House Select Intelligence Committee 

REFERENCE: Your tel econ with J. Kenneth McDonald, 

Chief, DCI History Staff 


25X1 


1. The History Staff has no general histories of Agency activities in 
Laos or Southeast Asia. 

2. The previous History Program produced a number of monographs 
covering individual components involved in or specific operations related to 
CIA actions in the areas in question. These are listed in an attachment, 
together with a brief description. If you feel that any of these histories 
meet the needs of the House Select Committee on Intelligence, access 
approval should be secured from the originating directorate, as indicated. 

3. Of the histories listed, Mo. 026, Vietnam and the Office of National 
Estimates, 1 951 -1 946 (TS) and No. 002, CIA-MEO Activities, T950-64" provide 
the best accounts of specific aspects of CIA's role in Southeast Asia.
